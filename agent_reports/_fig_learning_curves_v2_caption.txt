Figure X. Learning curves for evaluation success rate vs training steps,
faceted by Fetch env. Shaded bands show +/-1 SE across 3 random seeds. The
verified_cf cold-start regime is visually apparent: success remains near
zero from ~0--200k steps as the verifier rejects most VLM proposals, then
climbs steeply once policy precision rises. By 500k, verified_cf reaches
the same convergence-horizon SR as vlm_cf on Push/PnP and exceeds it on
Slide. The HER baseline is truncated at 250k steps (its pre-registered
horizon); Oracle-CF runs continue to 1M steps on PickAndPlace.
